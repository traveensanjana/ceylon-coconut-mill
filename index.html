What We Stand For

Three core values drive everything we do at Thelambugammana Oil Mills.

Purity First

Every batch of our coconut oil is processed without additives, preservatives, or shortcuts. What you get is exactly what nature intended: pure coconut oil.

Community Roots

We buy coconuts from local farmers at fair rates, support local employment, and reinvest in the community that has made us who we are.

Sustainable Practice

We turn every part of the coconut into something useful, from oil to animal feed to coconut shells, because we believe in responsible, low-waste production.
