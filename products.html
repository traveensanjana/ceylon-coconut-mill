Our Products

Pure coconut oil and valuable byproducts, crafted with care from locally sourced Sri Lankan coconuts.

Main Product

Our flagship offering is pure coconut oil, trusted by homes and businesses across Sri Lanka.

Pure Coconut Oil

Our coconut oil is cold-pressed from the finest Sri Lankan coconuts, sourced directly from local farmers. It contains no additives, no preservatives, and no artificial processing. Pure, natural coconut oil exactly as nature intended.

Ideal for cooking, hair care, skin moisturising, and traditional Ayurvedic wellness practices. Available in various quantities for households, grocery stores, and bulk commercial buyers.

Byproducts

Nothing from a coconut goes to waste at our mill.

Animal Feed

After extracting oil from coconuts, the remaining coconut cake is processed into high-quality animal feed. Rich in fibre and nutrients, it is used by local farmers to feed livestock.

Coconut Shells

The coconut shells produced during our milling process are made available to local industries for a variety of uses, from charcoal production to crafts and fuel.
