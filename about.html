About Us

A trusted Sri Lankan coconut oil mill, rooted in community and quality since 2002.

Our Story

Thelambugammana Oil Mills was established in 2002 in the heart of Thelambugammana, Yakwila, Sri Lanka. What started as a small, family-driven operation has grown into a trusted local business serving households, grocery stores, and small businesses across the region.

From day one, our mission has been simple: produce the purest coconut oil possible, support local coconut farmers with fair pricing, and give back to the community that has always been our foundation.

Our government licence, issued by Sri Lanka's Industrial Development Authority, is a mark of the standards we uphold every single day.

What We Stand For

Three core values drive everything we do at Thelambugammana Oil Mills.

Purity First

Every batch of our coconut oil is processed without additives, preservatives, or shortcuts. What you get is exactly what nature intended: pure coconut oil.

Community Roots

We buy coconuts from local farmers at fair rates, support local employment, and reinvest in the community that has made us who we are.

Sustainable Practice

We turn every part of the coconut into something useful, from oil to animal feed to coconut shells, because we believe in responsible, low-waste production.

Government Licensed & Trusted

Thelambugammana Oil Mills holds an official licence from the Sri Lanka Industrial Development Authority (IDA). This licence is a guarantee that our operations, production methods, and products meet the standards set by the Government of Sri Lanka.

Having operated under this licence since 2002, we are one of the region's most established and trusted coconut oil producers.
